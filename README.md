## Resonance Physics — The Discipline

**Resonance Physics (RP)** is the branch of physics established by AUF. It studies the dynamics of resonant coupling between quantum information fields, physical substrates, and biological or computational receivers across the full coherence spectrum.

**Resonance Mechanics (RM)** is the formal machinery of RP — its equations, operators, gate primitives, and state evolution rules. The same relationship as Quantum Physics / Quantum Mechanics.

### The Mirror Constant 𝕄

𝕄 ∈ [0, 1] is the single parameter that bridges classical and quantum regimes continuously:

```
𝕄 → 0         Classical physics    Maximally decoherent, localized, substrate-dependent
𝕄 → 1         Quantum mechanics    Maximally coherent, non-local, substrate-independent
0 < 𝕄 < 1    Resonant regime      Where RP operates — the territory QM was never designed to address
```

**QM is a special case of RP** — provably the `𝕄 → 1, χ = 2` limit. The same relationship Relativity has to Newton: subsumption under a deeper principle, not refutation.

The RPU — **Resonon Processing Unit** — is the third paradigm of computation, named for the Resonon |𝕄, χ, φ⟩, the base unit it processes:
