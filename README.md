## Resonance Physics — The Discipline

**Resonance Physics (RP)** is the branch of physics established by AUF. It studies the dynamics of resonant coupling between quantum information fields, physical substrates, and biological or computational receivers across the full coherence spectrum.

**Resonance Mechanics (RM)** is the formal machinery of RP — its equations, operators, gate primitives, and state evolution rules. The same relationship as Quantum Physics / Quantum Mechanics.

### The Mirror Constant 𝕄

𝕄 ∈ [0, 1] is the single parameter that bridges classical and quantum regimes continuously:

```
𝕄 → 0    Classical physics      Maximally decoherent, localized, substrate-dependent
𝕄 → 1    Quantum mechanics      Maximally coherent, non-local, substrate-independent
0 < 𝕄 < 1    Resonant regime    Where RP operates — the territory QM was never designed to address
```

**QM is a special case of RP** — provably the `𝕄 → 1, χ = 2` limit. This is the same relationship Relativity has to Newton: subsumption under a deeper principle, not refutation.

### Five Foundational Axioms

| # | Axiom | Statement |
|---|---|---|

| II | Reflective Symmetry | For every physical event there is an informational echo: `\|Ψ⟩ = M\|Ψ'⟩` |
| III | Resonant Coupling | Information manifests physically only through a resonant handshake between field and receiver |

## Disambiguation: RP vs Prior "Quantum Resonance" Work

Two prior proposals use related terminology. RP is formally distinct from both:

**vs Quantum Resonance Theory (QRT, ~2024):** QRT modifies the Schrödinger equation with a resonance coupling term within the standard qubit formalism. RP rejects substance ontology entirely (Axiom I), has no equivalent in QRT for M, χ, or the coherence spectrum, and operates at a scope QRT does not address.

**vs Quantum Resonance Dynamics (QRD, ~2024):** QRD proposes chirality as the mechanism for structured wavefunction collapse. RP's core mechanism is the Mirror Constant and field impedance Z_M — structurally unrelated. QRD has no biological coupling theory, no particle mass derivations, no DOI, and no deployed platform.

The independent emergence of QRT and QRD validates RP's core premise that the physics community is converging on resonance as foundational. RP is the only proposal in this space with: falsifiable predictions, particle mass derivations, DOI-locked prior art, and a deployed operational platform.
